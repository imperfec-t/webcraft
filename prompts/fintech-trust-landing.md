Build a landing page for [PRODUCT NAME], a [FINANCIAL PRODUCT, E.G. BUSINESS ACCOUNT / SAVINGS APP / PAYMENTS API] for [TARGET CUSTOMER] in [COUNTRY OR REGION].

Tone: calm, precise, trustworthy. Motion is restrained; clarity beats spectacle in finance.

Stack: [REACT + TAILWIND OR HTML/CSS]. Tabular figures for every number (font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums).

SECTIONS
1. Header: logo, Product, Pricing, Security, Company; "Log in" and primary "[CTA LABEL]".
2. Hero: headline [HEADLINE, 6 TO 10 WORDS], subline stating the concrete benefit [SUBLINE WITH A NUMBER, E.G. RATE OR SPEED], primary CTA plus "[SECONDARY CTA]". Right: a product mockup [CARD OR APP SCREEN] with a balance figure that counts up once.
3. Trust strip directly under the hero: [REGULATOR / LICENCE STATEMENT], [DEPOSIT PROTECTION STATEMENT IF APPLICABLE], [SECURITY CERTIFICATIONS], customer count [REAL CUSTOMER COUNT]. Only facts that are true and verifiable.
4. Key figures: 3 large numbers [FIGURES WITH LABELS], e.g. rate, fee, time to open. Each has a footnote marker linking to the legal note.
5. Features: 4 cards for [FEATURES], each an icon, title and one sentence; no jargon.
6. Transparent pricing: a fees table [PLANS AND FEES] with no hidden rows; "No fees for" checklist.
7. Security: how money and data are protected [SECURITY POINTS], with a simple diagram.
8. Testimonials or reviews: [REAL REVIEWS OR RATING SOURCE]. Omit if not available.
9. FAQ in plain language: [FAQ ITEMS], including fees, safety, and how to leave.
10. Footer with full legal disclosures [LEGAL DISCLOSURES], registered company details and risk warnings where required.

MOTION
- Hero balance counts up once over 1.2s; key figures count up when 70% visible, once.
- Cards fade up on first entry (0.5s). No parallax, no pinning, no autoplay video.

COLORS AND TYPE
- Light background [BACKGROUND COLOR], deep text [TEXT COLOR], a single trustworthy accent [ACCENT COLOR]. Success green and warning amber only for states.
- A neutral sans like [FONT FAMILY] with tabular numerals.

QUALITY BAR
- prefers-reduced-motion: numbers render at their final value, no fades.
- Every rate or claim has a dated footnote; never invent figures, licences or reviews.
- WCAG AA contrast including footnotes and table text; tables have proper headers.
- Fast: no heavy libraries; LCP under 2s. Forms (if any) have labels and clear errors.
